**Datura v1.001.001: renamed from Nightshade Stream.** Renamed to Datura; same app, same title ID, your settings stay.

## What changed

- **The name.** The PS5 home screen, the header, the About page, the update dialog, the stream's notifications and the on-screen keyboard say Datura, in all 31 languages. The About page keeps the Moonlight, Sunshine and ProsperoLight credits.
- **Updates.** The app asks [Oliv3rmoon/Datura](https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Datura/releases) for a newer release. The old address, Oliv3rmoon/Nightshade-Stream, forwards there, so Nightshade Stream 01.001.000 finds this release and **Update now** installs it over itself.
- **What stays.** The title ID (PPSA99002) and `/data/prosperolight`, so it installs over Nightshade Stream and keeps your PCs, pairing and settings. Streaming is as it was, built with the same measured settings. The release's files are named as before.

## Install

- `PPSA99002.zip`: choose **Update now** when Nightshade Stream or Datura offers this release, or close the app, extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99002` folder over your current one (`/data/homebrew/PPSA99002`). Your PCs, pairing and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99002.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Datura` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
